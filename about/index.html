Mingjie Yu

I’m Mingjie Yu, though most people know me as Jason. I photograph people, places, and the quiet details that give a moment its shape.

What began with photographing friends and family grew into an ongoing practice across portraiture, events, streets, and natural landscapes. My approach is observational and patient. Whether I am working with a couple, following a room through an event, or walking without an assignment, I look for gestures and relationships that feel unforced.

I am based in Toronto and available for portrait and event commissions across the GTA.
