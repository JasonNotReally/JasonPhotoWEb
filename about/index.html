Mingjie Yu

I’m Mingjie Yu, though most people know me as Jason. I photograph landscape and streets in China and Canada

I got into photography while studying at the University of Waterloo, when I started a little project to keep track of what was blooming in a garden and when. From there, I just kept taking photos. Sometimes it’s the light that makes me stop; other times, I come across something interesting and want to capture it. I also spent two and a half years as president of the UW Photography Club, where I enjoyed helping others find their own joy in photography. These days, I take photos around Toronto and wherever my travels take me.

I am based in Toronto and available for portrait and event commissions across the GTA.
